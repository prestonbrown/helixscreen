Fair point. Here's the fix

The weirdness comes from offsetting at the moment of contact. So don't. Offset only once the finger is already moving.

1 · Touch down
0,0
Target appears exactly under your finger. No jump, nothing to notice.
2 · Start moving
0,0
It lifts clear on a short stem while you are already in motion, so your eye is tracking it, not the gap.
3 · Lift
0,0
One move fires to the target. The head arrives where the dot was.
X 70.4   Y 44.9
Why this works: the offset never appears as a discontinuity. At contact there is none, and it grows over the first few millimetres of travel, by which point your attention is on the moving target rather than your fingertip. It is the same trick a text cursor uses on a phone. The one place it is still visible is a very short drag, so below a few millimetres of finger travel we treat it as a tap and skip the offset entirely.
