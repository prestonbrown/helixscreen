Fixes from 1.1. The crash screen always restarts HelixScreen, and a very large reply from the printer no longer leaves the screen offline. Saving a calibration keeps the new value, PRINT_START is found on more printers, and a K2 with no CFS works as a single-spool printer. Also fixes for Happy Hare speed settings, toolchangers with gaps in their tool numbers, fan detection, time zones and label printers.
