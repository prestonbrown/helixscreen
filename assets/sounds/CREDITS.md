# Sound credits

## pole_position_nights.mod

4-channel ProTracker module. The V10 engine sample is cut from
"Red Bull-Cosworth RB1 (2005).ogg" by Edvvc, Wikimedia Commons,
licensed CC BY-SA 3.0:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_Bull-Cosworth_RB1_(2005).ogg
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The sample is 15.5-24.5 s of the recording, high-passed, slowed to 80% tempo
at constant pitch, resampled to 11084 Hz and faded in and out.
